End‑to‑End Banking Payment Flow (EDI 820 → 997 → 824 → 821)

Step 1: EDI 820 – Payment Instruction

System: “A corporate has sent a payment request to the bank.”
Tester: “Let me verify what payment is being requested and whether it makes sense.”
Tester: “First, I check the amount, trace number, payer, payee, and invoice.” 
ST*820*0001~
BPR*C*12500.00*C*ACH*CTX~
TRN*1*PAYREF20260412~
N1*PR*CORP CLIENT INC~
N1*PE*ABC SUPPLIER LLC~
RMR*IV*INV20260315*12500.00~
Tester Validations:
  • Does BPR amount = invoice amount?
  • Is TRN unique?
  • Does vendor exist and have a valid bank account?

Step 2: EDI 997 – Functional Acknowledgment

Bank: “I received your file and it is structurally correct.”
Tester: “Good. But this does NOT mean payment is successful yet.” 
ST*997*0001~
AK1*RA*101~
AK2*820*0001~
AK5*A~
AK9*A*1*1*1~
Tester Checks:
  • Control numbers match EDI 820?
  • Accepted vs rejected?
  • Partial acceptance?

Step 3: Bank Processing (No EDI File)

System: “Bank is performing internal checks.”
Tester: “I check logs, cut‑off timing, ACH vs Wire routing.”
Tester Focus:
  • Was file before cut‑off?
  • Is this ACH or Wire?
  • Any compliance or fraud holds?

Step 4: EDI 824 – Application Advice (Business Decision)

Bank: “I have completed business validation.”
Tester: “Now I know if payment was accepted or rejected.” 
ST*824*0001~
OTI*TA*820*0001~
TED*005*PAYMENT ACCEPTED~
REF*TRN*PAYREF20260412~
Tester Checks:
  • Status accepted or rejected?
  • Error reason makes sense?
  • TRN matches EDI 820?

Step 5: EDI 821 – Bank Statement & Reconciliation

System: “Payment has been settled and reported.”
Tester: “This proves money actually moved.” 
ST*821*0001~
AMT*TT*12500.00~
REF*TRN*PAYREF20260412~
Tester Reconciliation:
  • Amount matches EDI 820
  • Trace number consistent
  • No missing or duplicate payments

End‑to‑End Tester Mindset

EDI 820 asks for money movement.
EDI 997 confirms file receipt.
EDI 824 confirms business decision.
EDI 821 confirms settlement.

A tester validates the journey of money — not just files.
